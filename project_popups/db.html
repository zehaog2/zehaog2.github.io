Overview

End-to-end restaurant analytics pipeline from raw visit data to validated business reporting. The project emphasizes system architecture: 3NF relational modeling, robust ETL quality controls, integrity-first MySQL implementation, and reproducible analytics reporting.

Engineering Highlights
  • MySQL DDL enforces relational integrity, uniqueness, and domain rules across all core tables
  • Stored procedures support operational inserts and create missing related entities before persisting visits
  • Automated validation compares row counts and FoodBill, AlcoholBill, TipAmount, and total revenue aggregates
  • Reproducible script-based workflow executes creation, loading, tests, business logic, and reporting end-to-end
