Overview

A probabilistic temperature forecasting system that ingests output from 21 NWP models across 100+ ASOS/METAR stations. Uses ensemble spread analysis and IQR-based model ranking to outperform NWS and commercial baselines on edge cases like marine-layer and boundary-layer regimes.

Technical Highlights
  • Ensemble spread analysis with IQR-based model ranking for dynamic weighting
  • 12 city-specific microclimate models covering boundary-layer, marine-layer, solar/stability regimes
  • Forecast reliability validated via freshness rules, staleness discounting, and geospatial cross-referencing
  • Demonstrated superior edge-case accuracy over NWS and commercial baselines
